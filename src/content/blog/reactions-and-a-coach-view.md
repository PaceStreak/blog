Two small social additions, both built so they can't be turned against
anyone.

## Reactions

Kudos on a feed post can now be one of five: 👏 kudos, 🔥 on fire, 💪 strong,
⭐ star or ❤️ love it. The smiley beside the kudos button opens the five; a
plain tap on kudos still gives kudos.

They are **presets only**. Nothing typed means nothing to moderate and nothing
that can carry a slur, a link or a phone number. Every reaction still counts as
kudos, it's still one per person per post, and changing your reaction replaces
it. The feed shows counts per reaction and which one is yours.

## A coach's overview

Coaching groups have existed for a while: a member who turns on sharing for
the group lets its coach see their actual sessions. Coaches with several
groups, though, had to open each one.

**Coaching** is now one screen listing everyone who shares their training with
you, across all your coaching groups: this week's progress against their
target, their streak, how far through a plan they are, and their last
session. Anyone who needs a nudge, because the week is at risk or nothing has
been logged for a week, is marked and sorted to the top.

The consent rule is exactly the one in each group, checked the same way: **no
share, no row.** Someone in your group who hasn't turned on sharing isn't
listed at all, not even as "private". Turning sharing off removes them from
the overview the moment they do. And like everywhere else in PaceStreak,
**habits never appear**, not even to a coach.

The screen only appears in your menu once you coach a group, so it's not
another empty page for everyone else.
