Open a habit app and the first thing you want to know is simple: what have I
done today, and what's left? The old Today screen answered it three cards down,
below a streak strip, a coach card and a getting-started list.

## A tear-off page

The top of Today is now a tear-off date block: the weekday on a red band, the
day number huge and condensed, the month under it. Beside it, your whole-life
chain, *6 weeks unbroken*, in red. It's the one number the product is built to
protect.

## This week, as a board

Directly under that: **This week**. One row per habit, the habit's name on its
own line, then seven boxes, Monday to Sunday. Today's column is ringed in red.
Done days carry a marker X in the habit's colour; part-done days a single
stroke; a slip a ring.

The key decision was making the whole week tappable, not just today. The most
common complaint about habit trackers is not being able to fix yesterday. Here
yesterday is a box you can see, so you tap it.

## Names get their own line

The first version put the habit name in a narrow column beside the boxes. On a
phone that squeezed "Drink water" to "D". The name now spans the full width
above its seven boxes, so both get the room they need.

## The coach moves down

The coach cards, suggestions and the getting-started list are still there,
below the board. They're useful, but they're not the first thing.
