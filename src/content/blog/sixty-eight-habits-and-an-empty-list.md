When PaceStreak grew beyond training, the question was what else people
actually keep. The answer is a catalogue of 68 templates:

- **Health**: water, sleep, steps, stretching, vegetables.
- **Learning**: reading, a language, an instrument, a course.
- **Mind**: meditation, journaling, gratitude.
- **People**: calling home, a message to a friend.
- **Focus, money, home and creative**: deep work, a budget check, ten minutes of
  tidying, drawing.
- **Breaking**: doom-scrolling, smoking, late-night snacking.

## Four kinds of habit

Each template is one of four kinds, because "did it" isn't the only shape:

- **Tick**: did it or didn't.
- **Count**: six glasses, twenty pages.
- **Time**: fifteen minutes of practice.
- **Break**: clean days, with slips logged.

Skills can carry a long goal, like fifty hours of a language, and the app shows
when you'll get there at your recent pace, with the year when it's more than a
few months out.

## The list starts empty

It would be easy to pre-fill someone's list with water, sleep and reading. It
would also be a list of things they didn't choose, and unticked defaults look
like failure on day one. So the list starts empty with one quiet invitation,
and the catalogue is there when you open it. At most 30 active habits: past
that, the list is a to-do list.
